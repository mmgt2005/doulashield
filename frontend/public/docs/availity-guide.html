Availity Setup Guide

Connect your agency to Availity for claims

Before DoulaShield can submit claims to Medicaid MCOs on your behalf, you need an Availity API account. Choose your path below to get step-by-step instructions.

Select your situation to see the right steps
Path A

Independent Doula Setup

Before you start: Have your personal NPI and Tax ID (or SSN if you're a sole proprietor) ready. Availity account creation usually takes 1–3 business days for verification.
1
Create your Availity account
Go to availity.com and click Register. When asked what type of user you are, select Provider.

You'll be asked for: your personal 10-digit NPI, your Tax ID (EIN) or SSN if sole proprietor, your practice name, address, and phone. Availity will verify your identity and send a confirmation email within 1–3 business days.
2
Log in and open Manage Keys
Once your account is approved, log in to Availity. In the top-right, click your name → My Account. In the left sidebar, find and click Manage Keys.
3
Create an API application
Click Create Application. Give it a name (e.g. "DoulaShield Claims"). When selecting scopes, check all of these:
837P Claims 270/271 Eligibility 276/277 Claim Status 835 Remittance
4
Copy your Client ID and Client Secret
After creating the application, Availity will display your Client ID and Client Secret. The Client Secret is shown only once — copy it immediately and store it somewhere safe. If you lose it, you'll need to create a new application.
5
Enter your credentials in DoulaShield
In DoulaShield, go to Settings → Availity Credentials. Enter your Client ID and Client Secret, then click Save. DoulaShield will use these to authenticate every claim submission.
6
Enroll with each MCO in Payer Spaces
Back in Availity, click Payer Spaces in the top navigation. Search for each Medicaid MCO you bill and request enrollment. This allows Availity to route your 837P claims to the correct payer. Expect 1–5 business days per payer for approval.

Path B

Agency / Billing Admin Setup

Before you start: This is your agency's single Availity account — not an individual doula's. You'll need your agency's Group NPI (10-digit) and EIN. Once set up, all doulas in your agency submit claims through your agency credentials.
1
Register your agency as a Provider Organization
Go to availity.com and click Register. Select Provider Organization (not "Provider").

Enter your agency's Group NPI (10-digit) and EIN, your organization name, address, phone, and a primary contact. Availity will verify your organization within 1–3 business days.
2
Log in and open Manage Keys
After approval, log in to Availity. Click your organization name in the top-right → My Account. In the left sidebar, click Manage Keys.
3
Create an API application for DoulaShield
Click Create Application. Name it (e.g. "DoulaShield Agency Claims"). Select all four scopes:
837P Claims 270/271 Eligibility 276/277 Claim Status 835 Remittance
4
Save your Client ID and Client Secret
After creating the application, Availity will display your Client ID and Client Secret. The Client Secret is shown only once — copy it immediately and store it securely (a password manager is ideal). If you lose it, you must create a new application.
5
Enter credentials in DoulaShield Billing Admin
In DoulaShield, go to Billing Admin → Settings → Availity Credentials. Enter your agency's Group NPI, Client ID, and Client Secret, then click Save.

All doulas linked to your agency will now have their claims submitted through your agency Availity account. Each claim appears in the Claims queue as "Pending Review" until you approve it.
6
Enroll with each MCO in Payer Spaces
In Availity, click Payer Spaces. Search for and request enrollment with each MCO your doulas bill. This must be done for the agency account — individual doula enrollments are separate. Each payer takes 1–5 business days to approve.
